About Thanganat 5.0

Swaminarayan University's marquee Navratri congregation returns in its fifth edition, bigger and more dazzling than ever. Hosted on the expansive New Cricket Ground, Thanganat 5.0 bridges contemporary campus camaraderie. Featuring star performer Kirtidan Gadhvi live on stage, whether you are stepping into the Dodhiya circle for the first time or competing for the coveted Winners Podium, the Raas ground awaits.

Dress Code Standards

Authentic traditional attire is mandatory for arena entry.

Gentlemen: Kedia, Kurta Pyjama, or Dhoti Kurta
Ladies: Chaniya Choli or Bandhani Sarees

Western wear & casual footwear restricted inside the inner circle.

Safety, Conduct & Zero-Tolerance Policy

Every attendee must present an unexpired Swaminarayan University physical/digital ID alongside their EventHive QR Pass. Metal detection, mandatory bag scans, and internal CCTV surveillance are active across the perimeter. Substance consumption of any kind will result in immediate disqualification and collective removal.
